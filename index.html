The shop

A quiet corner of Delft that is very good at coffee.

White walls, plants along the shelves, coffee gear and bags of beans for sale, nerdy coffee magazines left out on the table, and a long bench where people settle in for an hour.

A rotating single origin on filter, a house blend on espresso, and an owner who will happily talk you through either. Women-owned, and small — mornings are the calmest time.
